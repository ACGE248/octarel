# Cost and provider safety

- Choose role and workflow before provider; route to the least-expensive capable authorized worker.
- Persistent Google/xAI repository-data authorization does not authorize live/billable product calls, paid
  development APIs, overflow, spend-limit bypass, or a role/capability/health/worktree/cost safety bypass.
- Provider substitution preserves core, role, workflow, task contracts, acceptance criteria, and
  read/write capability. Record the actual worker/provider/model and reason.
- Codex Runbooks default to `conserve`; `balanced` and `unrestricted` retain ENG-AGENT-03's explicit
  eligibility, invocation limit, and audited operator-override rules.
- Quota/rate limits, provider outage, auth/CLI unavailability, and context limits do not prove a stronger
  model is needed. Use an equivalent eligible provider; minimize context before any context-limit retry.
- Reasoning/capability escalation requires bounded evidence. Safety, secret, authorization, worktree, and
  policy failures block without provider hopping.
- Never silently enable API-key billing, paid overflow, premium fallback, live product calls, or bypass
  enablement/capability/CC/spend/accounting/readiness controls.
