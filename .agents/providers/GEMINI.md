# Gemini provider adapter

Gemini through OpenCode2 is preferred for authorized low-cost mechanical tests, repository search,
research, simple diagnosis, and bounded exact-tree review. Its configured workers are read-only. Keep context to bounded task files,
diffs, tests, and excerpts. On quota/rate/context limits, record the failure and reroute equivalently or
minimize context; do not strengthen the model merely because quota was hit.

Configured Google development workers are persistently pre-authorized for minimized, redacted,
task-relevant Octarel and selected managed-project repository-data reuse without repeated per-task consent. This does not change
authentication, health, role/capability, permission, worktree, cost/concurrency, secret-exclusion, or separate
live/billable-call gates.
